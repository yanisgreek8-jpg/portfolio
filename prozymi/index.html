Method

Four days,
one loaf.

Industrial bread is finished in ninety minutes with commercial yeast and a dough improver. Ours takes four days because fermentation is what makes bread digestible, keeps it fresh without additives, and gives it the sour edge you actually came for.

01 —

Feed

The starter is refreshed twice a day, every day, at the same hours. It has not been allowed to go hungry since 2016.

02 —

Autolyse

Flour and water rest alone for two hours before salt touches them. The gluten builds itself; we barely knead.

03 —

Cold rise

Twenty-four hours at 4 °C. Slow and cold is where the flavour comes from — there is no shortcut for time.

04 —

Deck oven

Loaded by hand onto stone at 400 °C with a burst of steam. Twelve minutes covered, thirty open.

The bakehouse

Three rooms,
one starter.

The whole operation is forty square metres on Riga Feraiou street: a mixing bench, a retarder, and a stone deck oven that was second-hand when we bought it in 2016. Everything below happens between three and eleven in the morning.
