Document design

Margin Review — the same AI report, before and after design

A 2,500-word consulting report, twice. First exactly as a chatbot returns it and someone pastes it into Word: markdown hashes left in the text, tables as pipe characters, seven pages at one size with no cover and no page numbers. Then the same file after layout. Not one sentence of the report was rewritten — what changed is a cover, a contents page whose numbers are generated when the PDF renders, a narrow reading measure, seven typeset tables and five charts drawn from the numbers the text was already describing. Flip the two versions page by page, read all twelve pages on the site, or download both PDFs and check the words against each other.

Kestrel Lead Engine — what an automation actually does

A solar installer's enquiry form, wired end to end. Pick one of three enquiries, press Send, and watch all nine steps run in front of you with the real data moving through them — the phone number cleaned, the postcode resolved, the lead scored against five written rules, the owner buzzed on his phone in four seconds. A second tab is the part nobody demos: four ways it breaks, and what the scenario does about each one — retries, parks the lead, replays it, and writes to the owner in plain English instead of losing a customer quietly.
