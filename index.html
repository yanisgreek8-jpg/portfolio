How it works

Four steps, no guessing

You always know what happens next and what you get at the end of it.

1

Brief and questions

You send the content and the goal. I come back with a short list of questions so nothing important is missed.

2

Structure first

You approve a slide-by-slide outline or a page map before any design work starts. Cheap to change at this stage.

3

Design and build

I design in your colours and fonts, or propose a direction if you have none yet. You see the first full draft.

4

Revisions and handoff

Revisions are included. You get editable source files — PPTX and PDF, or the full HTML — not a locked export.
