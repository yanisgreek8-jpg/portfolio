What a spraying season actually costs

Three days for one pass

A tractor on a hillside plot needs most of a week to cover the rows. The weather rarely gives you most of a week.

A crew you cannot book

Manual spraying runs around 320 euro per operation, and every grower nearby wants the same people in the same fortnight.

Product on the ground

Ground equipment puts far more chemical into the plot than the vine takes up. You pay for that twice, at purchase and at runoff.

None of this is a spraying problem. It is a scheduling problem.
