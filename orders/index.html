Orders

Wholesale orders for the selected delivery date. Cafés and restaurants order by 18:00 the day before; anything confirmed by then goes into the night's bake plan.

Delivery list

Bake plan

Calculated from every confirmed order for this date. Nothing here is typed in by hand — change a quantity on an order and these numbers move with it.

Customers

Nine wholesale accounts around Patra. Volume and revenue are counted from the last 30 days of delivered orders.

This is a concept project. Prozymi Bakehouse, its customers and every figure on this page are invented for a portfolio piece. The application runs entirely in your browser — no server, no database, no account. Your changes are kept in this browser only, and Reset demo data in the sidebar puts everything back. Built with hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript: no framework, no build step, one file. The bakery's landing page is here, the rest of the portfolio here.
