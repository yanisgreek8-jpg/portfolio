The problem

Your treasury lives in nine spreadsheets and none of them agree.

Most funds still reconcile onchain positions by hand at month end. Meridian replaces that with one indexed source of truth — priced, attributed and exportable.

True yield attribution

Splits returns into fees, emissions, price and funding — so you can see which strategies actually earn and which are quietly subsidised by a token that is bleeding.

Liquidation headroom

Every leveraged position stress-tested against a 12%, 25% and 40% drawdown, with the exact price at which each loop unwinds. Alerts hit Slack before they hit your PnL.

Audit-ready exports

Cost-basis, realised and unrealised PnL per lot, in a format your accountant will accept without a phone call. CSV, Xero and a signed JSON attestation.

Risk engine

Find the cliff before the market does.

Meridian re-prices your whole book every block and runs the scenarios you would otherwise only run after something breaks.

—Correlated-collateral modelling. Looped LSTs are not diversified; we price them as one exposure, not four.
—Oracle-lag simulation. See what happens when the feed is 40 seconds stale and the pool is not.
—Depeg paths. Stablecoin exposure tested at 2%, 5% and 12% off peg, including secondary liquidity depth.
—Counterparty concentration. Flags when more than 30% of yield depends on a single contract.
