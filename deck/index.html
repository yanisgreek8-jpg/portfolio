About this deck

Meridian is an invented company — a treasury-analytics product for crypto funds — built so I could show a complete investor deck without touching a real client's confidential material. Every slide, chart and line of copy here is my own work.

The deck follows the structure investors actually expect: the problem stated plainly, why now, the product, market sizing, the risk model, traction, pricing and the ask. Charts are drawn to the same grid and colour system as the rest of the slides rather than pasted in from a spreadsheet.

Delivered the way I deliver every project: an editable PowerPoint file plus a print-ready PDF, so the team can update numbers themselves before the next meeting.
